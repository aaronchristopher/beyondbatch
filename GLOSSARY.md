# BeyondBatch

Customer commitments and the purchasing work needed to fulfill them.

## Language

**Customer request**:
A specific answer or deliverable owed to a customer, with one accountable PM. It may span several email threads and depend on several departments.

**Response obligation**:
An outstanding customer-facing answer or meaningful update with its own deadline. Fulfilling it does not necessarily resolve the underlying request.

**Purchase request**:
An internal proposal to acquire a defined quantity of a component by a required date. It is not an issued supplier order or an authorization to spend.

**Supplier purchase order**:
The authorized commercial order placed with a supplier. Its lines identify the components, quantities, units and applicable revisions being purchased.
_Avoid_: Customer PO

**Customer PO**:
The customer's purchase-order reference for finished goods or other customer deliverables. It is distinct from UniWell's purchase order to a supplier.

**Component requirement**:
The quantity and revision of a raw material, packaging item or intermediate needed for a particular operation at a stated time.

**Supply dependency**:
A link explaining which component requirement or purchasing work blocks a customer request or planned operation.

**Supplier commitment**:
A supplier's evidenced promise of a quantity and ship or arrival date. An internal forecast is not a supplier commitment.

**Receipt**:
A recorded physical arrival of a specific quantity and lot. Arrival does not establish ERP posting or quality release.

**Usable supply**:
Supply that satisfies the applicable identity, revision, inventory and quality requirements for a consuming operation. A released quantity is not necessarily unallocated.

**Allocation**:
A quantity assigned from a specific stock lot or expected delivery to a component requirement. Expected allocation is distinct from currently usable supply.

**Available-by date**:
The date by which a component must be eligible for its consuming operation. Required dock arrival may be earlier to allow receiving and quality work.
